# 🌿 PLDS – Product Lifecycle Decision Support

### Give Every Product a Better Future
**Repair or Replace**

PLDS is a web-based project designed to help users explore product health, compare repair and replacement costs, understand environmental impact, and make more sustainable product lifecycle decisions.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔐 **Login Page** – Demo sign-in interface with password visibility control.
- 💚 **Product Health Card** – Calculates an illustrative product health score.
- 🔧 **Repair vs Replace** – Compares repair and replacement costs.
- 🌱 **Eco Impact Score** – Provides basic sustainability guidance.
- ⚙️ **Failure Risk Analysis** – Estimates risk using product age, usage, and maintenance inputs.
- 🤖 **Lifecycle Chatbot** – Provides built-in answers about repair, maintenance, reuse, and recycling.
- 🌐 **Language Selection** – Offers basic language options.
- 📊 **Reports & History** – Stores assessment summaries and supports CSV export.
- 📱 **QR Product Passport** – Demonstrates a product passport interface with a decorative QR-style pattern.
- 🖼️ **Image Upload** – Allows users to select and preview product images.
- ℹ️ **About Page** – Explains the project purpose and available features.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
